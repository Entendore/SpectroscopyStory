FIRST LIGHT

A detective game about reading starlight. When a planet crosses its star, a whisper of its atmosphere imprints itself on the light — every molecule leaves a fingerprint. Eight assigned worlds, then the Deep Field: procedural planets without end. Some of them will lie to you.

01WATCH THE TRANSITStarlight filters through the planet's air on its way to you. That's the only moment the atmosphere is visible.
02MARK & MEASUREClick dips to log features; drag across a band to measure its depth against the noise corridor. Real detections escape the corridor.
03APPLY THE RULESThe disequilibrium monitor scores your atmosphere live. Some fingerprints are impostors — stellar lines, clouds, contamination, dead chemistry.
04FILE YOUR VERDICTAbiotic, inconclusive, or biosignature — and stake your reputation on the confidence you choose. Sometimes “inconclusive” is the bravest word in science.
